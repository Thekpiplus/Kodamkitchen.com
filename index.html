Branch 01

Downtown Ao Nang

KoDam Kitchen Branch 1 (Ao Nang Downtown)

A hidden gem on Khlong Hang Road — close to the market, wrapped in greenery, and favoured by travellers discovering Ao Nang beyond the beach strip.

  • No Parking Available Arrive by tuk-tuk, Grab, or on foot from downtown.
  • Popular for International Tourists A well-known stop for authentic Thai at a local price.
  • Lush Jungle Vibe Alfresco seating among potted greenery and quiet lanes.

KoDam Kitchen Branch 2 (Beachfront & Seaside)
